# Initial launch plan — premium operational consultancy

1 November 2026 · Requirements confirmed by Swayam. Planning update only; no application or deployment changes in this step.

## Release objective

A modern, credible portfolio for Swayam Singh, AI & Solutions Consultant, that generates qualified B2B inquiries from industrial and business teams. Communicate domain understanding and practical delivery through clear capabilities, relevant examples and a working inquiry channel. Retain the current studio-inspired aesthetic and framework.

## Initial release scope

1. **Hero:** clear identity, operational value proposition, Discuss Your Project and Explore My Work. Use one lightweight original industrial visualization; no mandatory animation before content.
2. **Four capabilities:** Operational Intelligence Systems; AI & Document Automation; Engineering & Process Automation; Custom Business Platforms & Integrations. Explain the workflow each addresses. Assessments, planning, deployment and support are process stages rather than an oversized service menu.
3. **Industry exploration:** Manufacturing, Construction, Real Estate and EPC; meaningful contextual links to available work. Manufacturing gets first priority; Construction/Real Estate equal prominence. Deep pages wait for approved content.
4. **Featured work:** concise, distinct MOS, Production Intelligence, Engineering Automation, Tender-to-Quotation and BOQ presentations. Each needs its status, problem, credible artifact/sample and conversation CTA. Preserve existing project routes; do not force long public technical disclosures.
5. **Approach:** complimentary 20-minute discovery; optional paid assessment for complex scope; scoped implementation, validation, deployment and ongoing improvement. No public fixed prices or universal duration claim.
6. **About:** independent consultant, Gujarat manufacturing/engineering experience, domain understanding and practical implementation. No confidential client identities, equipment counts, invented team, quantified outcomes or affiliations.
7. **Contact:** inquiry is primary; professional email and call booking are alternatives. Budget optional. No draft-only form presented as a working inquiry channel at release.

Keep research/specialized work as concise secondary credibility. Do not add new long-form sections merely to fill space.

Days are relative calendar days from implementation kickoff, not a guaranteed completion date. Owner review and configuration need to happen alongside development.

If contact configuration or project evidence is delayed, report the dependency and revised date. Do not silently launch with simulated delivery or false claims to satisfy the target window.

## 3D interaction contract

One original scene combines abstract structural geometry and recognizable infrastructure elements. It should explain information flow: an initial disconnected state → user chooses a workflow/industry → related nodes/paths connect → a plain-language explanation and relevant project become available.

Use explicit buttons with selected states, keyboard/touch access and the same explanatory text in the DOM. No hover-only requirement, scroll hijacking, mandatory orbit controls or autoplay tour. Keep the existing schematic/static view as fallback for reduced motion, loading failure or constrained devices. Defer heavy resources until after useful content; pause rendering when hidden/offscreen. Select the runtime only after a small prototype proves it earns its resource cost.

If full 3D misses its resource/quality gate, launch with the polished interactive schematic and schedule the richer render as the next enhancement. Obtain owner review of that tradeoff; never describe the schematic as a delivered realtime 3D experience.

## Evidence gate

Every featured project must show at least one approved evidence type: real redacted product capture, representative output, a working explicitly illustrative demo, or a reviewed architecture/workflow diagram. Illustrative evidence explains intended capability; it does not prove production deployment or delivered results. Pair it with exact current status and capability limits.

Preserve allowed project/capability states. Public display wording may follow the owner's supplied wording, but must not erase the distinctions between delivered components, active pilot/implementation, development, prototype, planned and research. The manufacturing experience statement is authorized; confidential specifics and unverified metrics are not.

## Inquiry behavior

Collect company/industry, business problem or project, current workflow/system, optional budget and preferred contact method, plus the contact details necessary to reply. Use a short free-text field for budget or agree optional brackets; do not infer public prices from private planning ranges. Phone is needed only when that method is selected. Timeline may remain optional.

Validate server-side, preserve input on errors, disable duplicate submission while pending and show success only after accepted delivery. Verify actual receipt in a controlled inbox. No personal details or project narratives in analytics. State the inquiry's purpose and relevant privacy/retention terms. Booking must point to a configured 20-minute appointment; no empty button or fake availability.

## Release verification and rollback

- Run production build, TypeScript, existing/new meaningful interaction tests and lint; report actual warnings.
- Verify homepage, project links, direct case-study requests and correct unknown-route handling on Vercel preview.
- Test valid/invalid inquiry, provider failure, retry, optional budget and preferred contact paths; confirm inbox receipt.
- Inspect phone/tablet/desktop, keyboard, focus, readable contrast, reduced motion, zoom/reflow and assistive-technology paths. No compliance claim without appropriate review.
- Measure production loading/interactions under documented conditions; no invented performance scores. Apply the existing provisional budgets and approve any tradeoff explicitly.
- Complete titles/descriptions, language, social preview and canonical/sitemap configuration using the actual deployed URL; update when domain changes.
- Keep the current static experience and separately reversible commits. Record the last verified hosted release and rollback procedure. Accepted inquiries must survive a presentation rollback.

Add these when content and business value justify them. Swayam should be able to maintain project summaries/statuses in the central typed content files without changing layout code.
